# City Thief: mission plan

Missions 1–20 (Chapters 1 and 2) are built and playable in the browser demo.
This plan covers what comes next: missions 21–40 (Chapters 3 and 4), side activities, and daily and weekly events.

## How every mission should feel (the "dopamine" rules)

1. **Clear goal, visible progress.** There is always a yellow GPS line, a counter ("3 / 8") or a bar.
2. **Instant rewards.** Cash pop-ups, combos, headshots, near misses, drift and stunt bonuses. Something good happens every 10–20 seconds.
3. **Surprise in the middle.** Every mission has a twist halfway: the target runs, a bomb starts ticking, the helicopter arrives.
4. **A big ending.** An explosion, slow motion, a boss going down or a jump into the sea.
5. **Stars to master it.** You pass with 1 star; 3 stars need speed, no damage or stealth. Players replay for 3 stars.
6. **Cliffhanger.** Each result screen teases the next mission in one line.
7. **Short.** 2–4 minutes per mission, so it fits a bus ride or a chai break.

## Side activities (play any time, with endless replay)

| Activity | How it works | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Street races (5 tracks) | Checkpoint races against 3 racers; best times saved | ₹1,000–5,000, a new car colour |
| Stunt jumps (12 hidden) | Find the yellow ramps; land the jump | ₹500 each; all 12 unlock a gold car |
| Rampage | 60 seconds, kill X gang members with a given gun | ₹ by combo; leaderboard |
| Taxi and auto-rickshaw jobs | Pick up passengers, drive smoothly, tips for speed | ₹200–800 per ride |
| Delivery rush | A GadgetGalli-style delivery: parcels across the city before the timer ends | ₹ + XP |
| Collectibles | 50 hidden "Navapur coins" on roofs and in alleys | Each gives ₹; all 50 give a golden SMG |
| Wanted challenge | Survive 5 stars for 3 minutes | Rank XP + title |

## Daily and weekly hooks

- **Daily reward:** done. A 7-day streak worth ₹500 to ₹5,000.
- **Daily challenges (3 a day):** for example "Get 5 headshots", "Drift 3,000 points", "Lose 3 stars".
- **Weekly event:** double cash on races, a special police chase, or a festival map (Diwali lights, Holi colours).
- **Season pass (later):** free track plus an optional paid cosmetic track. **No pay-to-win:** guns and upgrades are always earnable with game cash.

## Build order (suggested)

1. Night lighting and rain, which make every old mission look new.
2. Motorbike, then boat (needed for Chapter 3).
3. Sagarpet map: the harbour district first.
4. Missions 21–25, then 26–30.
5. Side activities: races, stunt jumps and taxi first (cheap to build, high replay).
6. Daily challenges.
7. Chapter 4.
