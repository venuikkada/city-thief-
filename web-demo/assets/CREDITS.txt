City Thief - browser demo: third-party assets

People (Vikram, Ravi, police, SWAT and 17 city people) + their motion-captured animations
  assets/people/*.glb - from Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library (MIT License),
  https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox
  Converted to glTF, textures shrunk to WebP, meshes simplified for phones.
  The police uniforms were recoloured khaki. Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation, MIT License.

Water
  assets/tex/waternormals.jpg - water normal map from the three.js examples (MIT License),
  https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js

Sky lighting
  sunset_1k.hdr - "Venice Sunset" HDRI from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0 / public domain.

Libraries: three.js (MIT), meshoptimizer decoder (MIT).

Everything else (the city of Navapur, buildings, seafront hotels, palm trees, cars, auto-rickshaws,
police SUVs, helicopter, guns, textures, shop signs, missions, sounds) is made in code for this project.
All names, places and police forces are fictional.
