City Thief - browser demo: third-party assets

Characters and animations (vikram.glb, citizen.glb)
  Source: three.js example models (Soldier.glb, Xbot.glb), https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js
  Characters and animations made with Adobe Mixamo; used under the Mixamo terms
  (free to use in games and projects; not to be redistributed as standalone assets).

Sky lighting (sunset_1k.hdr)
  "Venice Sunset" HDRI from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0 / public domain.

3D engine: three.js (MIT licence) - see js/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt

Everything else (city, buildings, cars, auto-rickshaw, police SUV, textures,
shop signs, sounds) is generated in code for this project.
All names, places and police forces are fictional.
