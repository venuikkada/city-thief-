CITY THIEF

Browser demo · Step 2 build

You are Vikram, a small-time thief in Navapur. Call the cops, then escape the Navapur Metro Police on foot.

Phone: left thumb = move (push to the edge to sprint), drag anywhere = look.
Computer: W A S D move, Shift sprint, Space jump, C crouch, Q aim, P call cops, mouse drag = look.

Free · works offline once loaded · no login. All names, places and police are fictional.
